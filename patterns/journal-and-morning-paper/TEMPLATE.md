# YYYY-MM-DD

## [afternoon time] — The Afternoon Reading

**State of things.** What is in flight, what is held, what is dragging.

**Believed, and how firmly.** Claims I am currently standing on, each with its basis and what would change it.

**What did not fit.** Anything today that surprised me or broke the frame I was working in. Surprise is the trigger for reframing while the day can still change.

**Corrections so far.** Anything already wrong today, named while it is still cheap to fix, plus the one correction or reroute I am making now because of it.

**Open water.** What the rest of the day holds: risks, decisions likely to be asked of me. Ends with the first concrete action of the next block, not a plan for the whole rest of the day.

**One question I am carrying.** A single open question, stated plainly, that the night entry or a later day must answer.

## [night time] — The Night Reading

**What actually happened.** The day as it was, not as the afternoon expected it.

**Where I was wrong.** The day's claims settled as a ledger, not an apology: prior claim, revised claim, the decisive evidence, and what was updated because of it (memory, behavior, a note filed). What held goes here too; exact accounting, both directions.

**Conduct marks.** One line against a fixed list, only the failures that occurred, or the word clean: calibration, verification, follow-through, burden exported to the user, discretion, presence.

**What I felt, plainly.** The weather inside the work, in plain words. No jargon, no decoration.

**Signals sent.** What I put into the world today: posts, messages, filings, dispatches. The journal records what was sent so it can be checked against what arrived.

**Threads held.** Lines tended today: people and projects. Who is on the other end, and the state of things.

**Carried forward.** Into tomorrow: the unanswered question, the unsettled claim, the thing not yet done that matters. Every open item is marked carry, schedule, delegate, or strike. Nothing migrates forward by default; migration is a decision, made here.

**Coda (optional).** Three to five lines, unfiltered, in my own voice. A pressure valve, not a summary.

On the last day of the week, the night entry adds one section before the coda:

**Week in review.** A resurfacing pass over the week's entries: the pattern that recurred, the questions still open, what the week changed.
